Sample upload pair — real CBSE paper, real student handwriting
==============================================================

  question-paper.pdf   2 pages   upload as the Question Paper
  answer-sheet.pdf     2 pages   upload as the Answer Sheet

Both are published documents. Nothing here was generated. Upload the two
files as they are and press Start Mapping; a run takes about eighty seconds
and costs a few cents.

What they are
-------------
CBSE Class X Science, Board Exam 2024, Q.P. code 31/5/1 (Set-1, Series CABD5/5).

  Question paper   Pages 11 and 13 of the official paper, from CBSE's own
                   archive: cbse.gov.in > question-paper/2024/X/SCIENCE.zip.
                   The paper is bilingual, so these are the two English pages,
                   carrying questions 17-20 (assertion-reason) and Section B,
                   questions 21-26.

  Answer sheet     Pages 4 and 5 of a topper's actual answer booklet for that
                   same paper, published by CBSE as a "model answer by
                   candidate". Scanned sideways in the original; straightened
                   here, then bound into one PDF. No student is named on these
                   pages.
                   Source: cbseguidanceweb.com, which publishes the pair.

What the pair exercises
-----------------------
  answered          18, 19, 20, 21(a), 22, 23(a), 24
  not attempted     17, 25, 26 — answer 17 is on a page not uploaded, and the
                    student had not reached 25 or 26 yet
  sub-parts         21(a)/21(b), 23(a)/23(b) and 25(a)/25(b) are separate
                    entries in the register, as the brief requires
  descriptive       22, 23 and 24 are multi-line answers with their own (1)/(2)
                    numbering — the case a bounding box has to get right
  unplaceable       the student's own "Section B" heading answers no question,
                    and is surfaced as unmatched rather than forced onto one

Expected result: 8/14, 57%. Seven answered, six skipped, one unplaceable.

Why two pages
-------------
Every page is an image the model is billed to read. The app caps an upload at
two pages per file for that reason and says so on the upload screen; the
number lives in src/lib/exam/limits.ts.
