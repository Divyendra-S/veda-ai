Q3. Chloroplasts are the site of photosynthesis. Main pigments are chlorophyll a, chlorophyll b and carotenoids. The two stages are:

  • 1. Light reaction — captures light energy and makes ATP and NADPH.
  • 2. Dark reaction (Calvin cycle) — uses that energy to fix CO2 into glucose.
